# Student Home Structure

Reference: `/alumnoWebSum/v2/inicio` (observed in the authorized student session before the browser redirected to login).

- **Application shell:** fixed-width dark blue sidebar at left; main column begins at x=280.
- **Sidebar:** student navigation with Inicio, Mi Información, Matrícula, Reportes, Mis Asistencias, Mi Tutoría, Plan de Estudios, and Manuales y Tutoriales. The first three group headings include `#` links in the initial state; child destinations were not inspected.
- **Header:** menu toggle on the left, elapsed-session clock near the center, student account control and Salir on the right. Student identity values are intentionally omitted.
- **Main content:** student summary at top with role and academic attributes, followed by eight vertically stacked shortcut cards. Card groups cover personal academic information, academic history and grades, current enrollment, course schedules, pre-enrollment report, enrollment report, debts, and study plan.
- **Card structure:** category label, section heading, short bullet list describing available information, and a “Ver más...” button. Destinations and resulting states remain unverified.
